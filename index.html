About Me

Hi! My name is Carolyn Kennett. Welcome to my portfolio.

I graduated from Texas Christian University with a Bachleor of Science in Geography. I have three minors: History, Business, and Digital Culture & Data Analytics.

In June 2025, I became a Support Analyst at Esri on the Data and Services team. Here are my main commitments in the role:

  • Provide technical support to users working with ArcGIS Online
  • Troubleshoot complex problems related to ArcGIS Online, like system errors and unexpected behavior
  • Reproduce and log ArcGIS product defects and enhancements
  • Write ArcGIS Online knowledge articles to help users and colleagues
  • Work closely with customers to understand their workflows and help them use Esri’s mapping technology

I also enjoy hand-coding websites with HTML and CSS. Fun fact: I hand-coded this portfolio, and this is my first website hosted on GitHub.
